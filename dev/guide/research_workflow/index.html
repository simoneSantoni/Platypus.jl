Reproducible research workflow

Platypus measures relative statistical structure in a supplied collection. Its scores describe that collection and chosen features. They are not probabilities of anomaly, hypothesis tests, historical first appearances, or causal effects. The workflow below concerns numerical and statistical validation; it does not attempt to measure human perceptions.

Declare the collection and features

Record the data source, version, inclusion rules, observation period and missing data decisions. Charting songs, indexed papers and surviving firms are selected populations: local scoring cannot recover entities absent from the source. Reference membership weights, recency weights and sampling weights encode different substantive choices. State what the chosen weights mean.

Always select measurement features explicitly in outcome studies. Numeric discovery includes numeric years, outcomes and controls unless excluded by an explicit feature_cols list.

using Platypus, DataFrames

df = DataFrame(id=1:8, year=[2020,2020,2020,2021,2021,2021,2022,2022],
    energy=[.1,.2,.4,.3,.6,.7,.8,.5],
    acousticness=[.8,.7,.5,.6,.3,.2,.1,.4],
    streams=[100,120,200,180,300,350,400,250])
measurement_features = [:energy, :acousticness]
cohort = df[df.year .<= 2021, :]
# The package rejects missing and nonfinite features. Here the decision is
# explicit complete-case selection; record and inspect the exclusions.
complete = dropmissing(cohort, measurement_features)
es = load_entities(complete; id_col=:id, feature_cols=measurement_features)
result = fit_typicality(build_pairs(es); method=:kde,
    provenance=(dataset="illustrative-v1", cohort="year <= 2021", cutoff=2021,
                preprocessing="complete cases on energy/acousticness",
                excluded_ids=setdiff(cohort.id, complete.id)))
scores = summarytable(result)
metadata = analysis_metadata(result)

Save metadata with the exported score table and the script, input checksum and resolved dependency environment. Metadata includes the transformation, normalized reference weights, effective sample size, method parameters, version and the supplied provenance. It records your declarations; it does not verify the source data or the accuracy of a declared cutoff. Join by entity_id, verify the join is one-to-one, and check outcome alignment after filtering or sorting.

Dropping incomplete observations changes the reference collection. Imputation also changes its geometry; document the method and fit preprocessing within the permitted time window. Compare reasonable preprocessing choices when missingness is appreciable. There is no built-in missing-data model.

Keep time explicit

Feature ranks, neighborhood structure and final percentiles are computed from the supplied collection. Pooling years changes the historical comparison by including later entities. Recompute separate dated cohorts or chronological prefixes when the research question requires information available by a cutoff. Construct the cohort before load_entities and build_pairs so future values cannot influence marginal ranks.

A prefix ending at a focal entity includes that entity in the endogenous collection; depth and copula fitting can include it, and transformations use the declared reference weights. This is not automatically a strictly prior reference or a model for scoring previously unseen observations. Platypus deliberately has no external-reference API. A strict historical prediction design needs additional methodological work; fit_outcome does not establish such a design from scores. Askin and Mauskapf's yearly measure explicitly compares against the preceding 52 weeks, a distinct temporal specification. Primary paper

Percentiles in independently fitted cohorts are relative to different reference distributions. A score change need not mean an entity's features changed. Retain cohort IDs and avoid treating these values as a fixed absolute measurement scale.

Interpret summaries and outcome fits

atypicality is one minus median pairwise typicality. novelty is a lower-tail typicality score: smaller means more unusual. It is not a minimum, and one unusual pair can leave the median almost unchanged. dispersion is variation across feature-pair scores, not a standard error.

LOF measures density contrast with neighbors; kNN and KDE measure neighborhood crowding or density; Mahalanobis depth measures global centrality. Copula fits impose their own dependence assumptions. Choosing between them changes the measurement target, not merely its computational implementation.

fit_outcome is a descriptive least-squares fit. It does not provide causal identification, automatic control selection, historical validity or calibrated uncertainty. Pair rows share features, and entity scores share the fitted reference collection. Do not treat scoretable rows as independent observations. For inferential work outside this package, choose resampling and clustering from the research design (for example artist, author or firm and time), and refit the measurement pipeline within each valid resample. Resampling only fixed score rows omits uncertainty from estimating the reference collection.

Check recovery and stability

The executable validation/experiments.jl compares all four typicality methods and both copula families on fixed seeded collections. It reports AUROC, threshold-based average precision, tie-aware precision at the planted anomaly count, row-permutation stability and sensitivity to added irrelevant features. The labels are generated from known numerical structure. Independent-feature and higher-order-only negative controls are retained even when methods fail.

julia --project validation/experiments.jl

The separate graph supplement uses those same generated collections and reports only graph atypicality, a full-feature connectivity target. It does not rerun the pairwise comparisons or invent a graph novelty summary:

julia --project validation/graph_experiments.jl

This is a small diagnostic experiment, not a universal method ranking or a test of predictive generalization. Different methods share a percentile output scale but target different structures. Bivariate density targets do not identify a joint constraint when every bivariate margin is identical to the comparison population. Inspect individual pairs and compare feature schemas, neighborhood sizes, bandwidths and reference definitions. A useful validation report explains both successes and deliberate failure cases.

The design follows the methodological lesson of varying anomaly frequency, cluster structure and feature relevance rather than reporting one convenient example; the supplied experiment covers a small subset of those dimensions. Emmott et al.
